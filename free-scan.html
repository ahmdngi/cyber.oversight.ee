Every commercial vessel carries four distinct network layers. The scan checks all four, from the mast to the engine room.

Layer 01 · Mast

Satellite uplink

Inmarsat and VSAT terminals answering on public satellite IP ranges, often with default factory credentials.

Inmarsat · VSAT · ASN
