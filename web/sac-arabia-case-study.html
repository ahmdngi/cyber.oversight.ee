// SAC Arabia Case Study

Corporate Oil & Gas B2B website for a Saudi Arabia-based contractor. Scope: rebuild of a 26-page legacy site into a 29-page modern build with full technical SEO.

29
pages
8
service detail pages
4
sector pages
SEO foundation
JSON-LD structured data (Organization schema), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, unique meta titles and descriptions per page, canonical and OG tags, geo tags for regional relevance.
Performance
Async font loading, preloads, lazy-loaded images, CSS cache-busting, and kebab-case URL cleanup across all cross-references.
Accessibility & correctness
Single H1 per page, descriptive alt text, fixed duplicate navigation, working contact form, and Google Maps integration on the address.
Design
Deep navy and gold palette for a serious B2B industrial presence, Bootstrap 5, service and sector detail pages, team, testimonials, and blog sections.
