01 · What the node does

Built for the bridge rack, not the data center.

Commercial vessels run on low bandwidth, intermittent satellite links, and strict safety rules. Haris was designed around those three constraints from the first board.

STAGE 01 // INGESTION

Collects

Ingests NMEA 0183/2000 and Signal K from the bridge instruments: position, heading, engine telemetry, and the sensors that keep the watch informed.

→ NMEA 0183 · N2K · Signal K
STAGE 02 // AIR-GAP BUFFER

Buffers

Thirty days of solid-state offline storage. When the satellite link drops, nothing is lost; when it returns, the data catches up seamlessly.

→ 30-Day Buffer · Solid State
STAGE 03 // ADAPTIVE UPLINK

Syncs

Compressed telemetry delivered over any link: VSAT, Inmarsat, or 4G in port. Webhook and sync targets on shore, with zero bandwidth drain.

→ Webhook · Sync · Compressed

03 · The crew app

Haris on the bridge, in the crew's hands.

The node collects. The app acts. Haris Crew App is a Flutter application that gives bridge officers plain-language, offline steps when something on board looks wrong.

01

Offline by design

The playbooks live locally on the device. Zero cloud dependency and zero satellite link needed when the communication link itself is the primary target under attack.

→ Works 100% Offline · Local Encrypted Store
02

Plain language steps

Written for officers who have a vessel to steer: isolate this device, disconnect that trunk, switch to secondary compass. Zero jargon, maximum actionable clarity under sea stress.

→ No Cyber Jargon · Action-Oriented Steps
03

Backed by a 24/7 desk
