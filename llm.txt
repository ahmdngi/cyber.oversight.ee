# Cyber Oversight - Maritime, OT Cybersecurity, Networking & Digital Infrastructure

Cybersecurity consultancy, managed infrastructure, enterprise networking, and corporate web engineering for maritime, industrial, and global enterprises.

## Products & Core Services

- Shipcrawler - vessel OSINT investigation platform. MMSI/IMO lookup, AIS tracking, port call history, Shodan scanning, Equasis, red-team playbooks.
- Haris - vessel edge data collection and transmission (NMEA 0183/2000, Signal K, compression, webhook/sync).
- M-SOC - Maritime Security Operations Center - continuous monitoring, threat hunting, and alerting for vessel fleets.
- Watchtower - vessel edge device management and monitoring.
- Haris Crew App - Flutter-based crew management application.
- Networking & Infrastructure - Enterprise LAN/WAN design, firewall hardening, VPN tunnels, SD-WAN, and 24/7 network monitoring.
- Web Design & Identity - High-conversion corporate websites and full visual identity systems for EU and GCC enterprises.
